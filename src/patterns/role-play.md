Role play is a powerful tool that enhances the practical application of knowledge and fosters the development of communication skills, empathy, and problem-solving. By immersing learners in simulated real-world scenarios, roleplay allows them to apply theoretical knowledge, understand concepts in context, and prepare for real-life situations. Learners can actively participate in sessions, learn through observation, and engage in debriefing and discussion to enhance their performance and interpersonal dynamics.  These experiences not only build confidence but also provide exposure to skills such as negotiation, conflict resolution, and customer service. Engaging in role play equips students with competencies directly applicable to their future careers.
