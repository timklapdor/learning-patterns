A study plan aims to get learners to set milestones for their learning and to check their progress throughout the course. This type of review provides learners with a sense of responsibility and accomplishment to personalise their learning journey throughout the course. To do this, learners will have to be able to assess their current knowledge and have a clear understanding of what kind of milestones learners should record. Any milestones should align the assessments and course learning outcomes with the learner's goals.
