Learner-developed and facilitated workshops provide an assessment method that facilitates practical application and promotes collaboration and teamwork through active learning. A workshop is an interactive learning environment where learners actively participate in hands-on activities, discussions, and problem-solving. It enables learners to enact their understanding of a topic by developing ways of facilitating activities with their peers that develop their own learning and provide opportunities to engage in their specific and unique perspectives.  Workshops can be based on diverse and individualised knowledge areas, skills-based activities, tutorial sessions, professional discussions or creative techniques. They provide a unique way of developing interpersonal skills and allow the shift of perspective from learner to teacher that can demonstrate the relevance of their learning and field of study.
