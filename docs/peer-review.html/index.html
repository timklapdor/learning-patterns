peer review

Learners provide a review of each others' work and discuss how that learner has applied their knowledge to a task.

Peer Review enables learners to step outside their work to see how others tackle a task. Peer Review requires instruction to guide learners to review aspects of another learner's work. For example, how has the other learner structured their work or how have they applied a concept? They must then be able to provide relevant and meaningful feedback. There also needs to be ground rules with this activity to ensure learners are constructive in their review and how they give feedback to each other. It may be helpful to provide learners with some additional resources or examples about how to provide feedback.
