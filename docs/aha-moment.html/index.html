aha moment

A clear intent to bring a concept together for the learner to connect the dots.

An Aha moment is a planned point in the course where learners unlock a critical understanding or relationship in the course. While learners may have these moments on their own, the aim is to construct a point in the course where you, as the teacher, create a learning sequence with the specific objective of learners experiencing a breakthrough. This can be done by connecting learners to a new idea in a learning sequence, marking a point where they level up their knowledge and expertise or discovering something new in the course.
